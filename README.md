# Hospital Management App

## Overview

The **Hospital Management App** is a robust mobile application designed to enhance the efficiency of hospital operations. This app centralizes key functionalities, enabling seamless coordination among various hospital teams. It ensures transparency, accountability, and data accuracy through advanced features like GPS live location tracking, expense management, and team-specific dashboards.

## Features

### General Features
- **Login/Logout Tracking**: Automatically records work hours for better time management and compliance.
- **GPS Live Location Monitoring**: Tracks real-time locations of team members during their tasks to enhance coordination and transparency.
- **Expense Management**: Logs travel, food, and other expenses with an option to upload receipts for streamlined reimbursements.

### Team-Specific Dashboards
Each team has a dedicated dashboard tailored to their specific roles and responsibilities.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d38461d-c9ec-4556-aa9e-fb591d66b8b5)

#### Scrub Team Dashboard
- Tracks surgeries, including the type of surgery and the surgeon’s name.
- Logs hospital visits with photo uploads for validation.
- Manages expenses, allowing users to record travel modes and upload receipts.
  
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e1543e3c-bbb3-46a0-b08c-9a659d33a472)

#### Collection & Billing Team Dashboard
- Records hospital visits and logs payment methods, including cash and cheque details with photo uploads.
- Manages billing details like invoice numbers and patient details for streamlined financial documentation.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4316d38a-bcb2-41dd-83ea-3d5b1c1598f0)

#### Runner Team Dashboard
- Logs delivery and pickup tasks with photo uploads for proof of completion.
- Tracks expenses related to travel and meals, ensuring clear documentation.
  
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/669183e6-2205-4648-8a65-421417876e33)

#### Sales Team Dashboard
- Logs hospital visits and interactions with surgeons for better follow-up.
- Tracks transport, journey, and meal expenses with attached receipts for accurate reporting.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b7d1a02-8160-4f30-aae6-d40a32d30ddb)


### Additional Features
- **Streamlined Role Selection**: Users can select their roles at login to access customized features.
- **Camera Integration**: Enables users to upload images of receipts, challans, and hospital documents directly from the app.
- **Secure FTP Integration**: Stores uploaded files such as travel receipts and hospital visit proofs on a secure FTP server.

## Technology Stack

- **Flutter**: For creating natively compiled, feature-rich, cross-platform applications.
- **Dart**: Language powering the app's core functionalities with efficient asynchronous operations.
- **Firebase**: Provides user authentication, real-time database, and cloud storage.
- **Android Studio**: For testing and debugging on virtual devices.
- **Visual Studio Code**: Main IDE for development, integrated with Flutter extensions.
